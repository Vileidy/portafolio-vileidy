# Brief: realistic product photo of a corporate T-shirt

Brief que se usó con Codex para generar `tm-camisa` (mockup de la camisa de Tumedico.com).
Referencias que se adjuntaron: el diseño plano original (`P.OP/camisa.jpg`, ampliado) y una composición de apoyo.

You have two reference images attached:

1. `ref1-diseno-plano.png` is the flat vector design of the T-shirt (front on the left, back on the right).
   It is the source of truth for colors, shapes and print placement.
2. `ref2-composicion.jpg` is a rough illustrated mockup that shows the desired layout and framing
   (front and back side by side, slightly rotated, soft shadows). Match the layout, not its flat look.

## What to generate

One photorealistic studio product photograph, landscape (about 3:2), of the **same T-shirt, front and back,
laid flat side by side** on a clean, softly lit, very light blue / pastel surface (like paper or matte table).
Soft natural light from the top left, gentle realistic fabric wrinkles and folds, visible cotton texture,
real stitching on collar, sleeves and hem, soft contact shadows. It must look like a real photo, not an illustration.

## Must stay exactly as in the design (do not invent anything)

- Ringer T-shirt: **white body**, **light sky-blue sleeves**, **navy-blue ribbed crew collar**, **navy-blue sleeve cuffs**
  and **navy-blue hem band**.
- Front chest print: the wordmark **Tumedico.com** in bold condensed navy-blue letters, a thin navy line under it that ends
  in a small light-blue heartbeat (ECG) pulse, and below it in italic the words **Salud en línea**.
- Back print, upper back, centered: the text **www.tumedico.com** and below it a row of three small round social icons
  (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).
- Spell every word exactly as written above. No extra logos, no extra text, no watermarks, no people, no props.

## Output

Save the final image as `out/camisa-realista.png` in the current directory, and tell me the exact path of the file
you saved (and of any other generated image file).
